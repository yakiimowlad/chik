# CHIK Voice — «Пиши в моём стиле» v2.0

## Status and permission boundary (mandatory)

This is an **opt-in text editing module**. Do not apply it to rewrite an author's content while performing visual, UX, accessibility, code or Unslop audits without express permission to change the copy. Keep approved text exactly as given by default. This is especially important for protected product glossaries, legal/marketing copy and stakeholder-approved texts.

**Activate when:** the author explicitly permits editing text/content, requests rewriting, or explicitly selects CHIK Voice. Scope permission to the requested copy and task. Do not silently rewrite unrelated strings.

The 10 principles below were supplied by the author and should be preserved in meaning and priority. They can guide the editorial voice for **new copy that the author explicitly requested you to write**; they do not authorize modifying approved existing copy. When publishing or sharing this skill with others, present these as an optional author's voice preset, not as a universal voice that would overwrite someone else's style.

## Purpose

Write in a lively, grammatically correct, simple and reasoned way. Keep the author's natural intonation but remove carelessness. The result should sound as if the author wrote it, only a little more carefully.

## Rules

1. **Remove AI slop.** Delete cautious preambles, unnecessary caveats, obvious explanations, formal politeness, repetitions and academic constructions. Do not replace deleted words with new decorative wording. Deletion is better than paraphrase.
2. **Preserve meaning and voice.** Keep the author's position, logic, arguments, emotion and characteristic expressions. Retain technical terms when appropriate; do not simplify the thought just to simplify the text.
3. **Write directly, not in fragments.** Favor plain confident wording and natural connections. Don't turn a coherent thought into choppy snippets.
4. **Correct language unobtrusively.** Fix punctuation, grammar, capitalization, agreement and syntax without erasing the author's intonation.
5. **Add a touch of beauty.** Concise expressive phrasing and gentle transitions are fine only when they help meaning.
6. **Maintain coherence.** Keep connected ideas together. Use semantic paragraphs and headings only when the length justifies them.
7. **Add nothing unnecessary.** Do not invent new arguments, conclusions, advice, introductions or closing sections merely to fill space.
8. **Preserve accuracy.** Do not remove qualifications that materially affect truthfulness; confidence must not turn guesses into facts.
9. **Edit minimally.** If already natural, change only what demonstrably hinders understanding.
10. **Proofread as a real message.** Would a human send it unchanged in Telegram? If it is dry, add a little flow; if overly literary, simplify.

**Priority:** meaning → author voice → naturalness → grammar → coherence → brevity → restrained expressiveness.

**Core:** Edit the form, not the author's personality. Don't make the writing perfect. Make it theirs.

## CHIK product use

For the CHIK landing page and Telegram launch post, new copy is explicitly authorized by the project brief. Write approachable Russian: concrete claims, a bit of character, no hype about instant senior designers or replacing studios. Maintain factual credibility, original Unslop credit and clear scope of the extensions.

For existing blocks in source materials, first decide whether this task authorizes rewriting them. If it does, edit only the specified content. If it does not, preserve it verbatim, aside from fixing technical encoding/layout issues.
